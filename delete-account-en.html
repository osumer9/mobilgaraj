Delete your Mobil Garaj account and data

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Türkçe

You can permanently delete your Mobil Garaj account and all data linked to it at any time. There are two ways to do this.

Option 1: In the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Mobil Garaj app and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap More in the bottom menu, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. In the confirmation dialog, type the requested word (DELETE) and tap Delete my account.

Your account and all your data are deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2: By e-mail

If you cannot use the app, send an e-mail from the address registered to your account to mobilgaraj.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Mobil Garaj account deletion request". Once we have confirmed that the account is yours, the request is carried out within 30 days and you are informed.

Data that is deleted

  • Your account: e-mail address, password and display name
  • All your vehicles and their odometer history
  • Maintenance records and plans, issues
  • Fuel and charging records, expenses
  • Toolbox and reminders
  • All photos and documents you uploaded (from the server within 24 hours)
  • The cache on your device (when deleted from within the app)

Shared vehicles

Maintenance, issue, fuel and odometer records you added to a vehicle someone shared with you stay with that vehicle; expenses and reminders you added to it are transferred to the vehicle's owner. Your private documents are not transferred to anyone and are deleted; files you shared on that vehicle are deleted too. When your own vehicles are deleted, the private documents of their other members stay with those members.

Data that is kept

After deletion, no data belonging to your account is knowingly kept. Deleted data may remain in the infrastructure provider's automatic backups for up to 30 more days and is then deleted from the backups as well.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

To keep your account but delete some data, delete the record (e.g. a fill-up, an expense or a document) or the whole vehicle in the app. Deleting a vehicle also deletes all its records and files. You can leave a shared vehicle at any time.
