Personal vehicle management app

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains what data the Mobil Garaj Android app (package name com.mobilgaraj) processes, why and how it is stored, and what your rights are. By using the app you accept this policy.

1. Data we collect

The app stores only the data you enter and the data it needs to work:

  • Account details: your e-mail address, an optional display name and your password. The password is stored only as a one-way hash; nobody, including the developer, can see it.
  • Sign in with Google: if you use "Continue with Google", Google shares, with your consent, only your name, e-mail address and profile picture link; no password is created. No other data in your Google account (e-mails, contacts, Drive, etc.) is accessed.
  • Vehicle details: make, model, year, trim, engine, transmission, fuel type, optional licence plate and chassis number (VIN), odometer, purchase date and price, notes.
  • Your records: maintenance and maintenance plans, issues, fuel fill-ups, electric charges, expenses, toolbox and reminders.
  • Files you upload: vehicle photos and the photos or PDF documents you choose (e.g. registration, insurance policy, invoice). These may contain personal information you add.
  • Vehicle sharing: when you share a vehicle or join one, who is on which vehicle and with which role (editor, viewer) is stored. Invitation codes themselves are not stored, only their one-way hashes. Failed code attempts are kept for one day to prevent abuse.
  • Preferences: your language, units and currency are stored only on your device.

What we do not collect: location, contacts, call logs, camera access, advertising ID, usage analytics or crash reports. The app has no ads and no third-party trackers. Photos and documents are only the files you pick with the system picker; the app does not otherwise access your gallery.

2. Why we use the data

The data is used only for the app's features: signing in to your account, storing your records and syncing them between your devices, calculating fuel and charging consumption and expense summaries, and showing maintenance and reminder notifications. Your data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with third parties.

What you share: if you share a vehicle with other users through an invitation code, that vehicle's details and records (maintenance, issues, fuel and charging, odometer, vehicle expenses and reminders) and the files you choose to share become visible to them. Files marked private are visible only to you. Members of the same vehicle see each other's display name and a shortened form of their e-mail address (e.g. o***@example.com). You can end sharing at any time.

3. Where the data is stored and how it is protected

  • The data is stored with the app's infrastructure provider Supabase (database, authentication and file storage). Supabase processes the data only on our behalf and to provide the service.
  • The servers are in the European Union, in Germany (Frankfurt).
  • Until 15 October 2026 the servers were in South Korea (Seoul); the data was moved to Frankfurt on that date. The copy left in Seoul is deleted by 15 November 2026 at the latest.
  • All connections between the app and the server are encrypted with HTTPS (TLS).
  • The server enforces row-level access rules: each user can access only their own data and the data of vehicles shared with them, according to the role the vehicle's owner gave them.
  • Files are kept in a non-public store and can only be opened by signed-in, authorised users.
  • When a vehicle's sharing ends, that person's access on the server ends immediately and the copies in the app are removed at the next sync. Copies that were already downloaded, saved or shared with other apps cannot be recalled.
  • A copy of the data is cached on your device for offline viewing. It is deleted when you sign out.

4. Notifications

Maintenance and reminder notifications are created locally on your device. No data is sent to an external server for notifications. You can turn notification permission off at any time in Android settings.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Your data is kept as long as your account exists.
  • Records you delete in the app are removed from the server immediately, files within 24 hours.
  • You can delete your account: More → Settings → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, all your vehicles, records and files. Records you added to someone else's vehicle stay with that vehicle; expenses and reminders you added to such vehicles are transferred to the vehicle's owner. Your private documents are not transferred to anyone; they are deleted. For details, and for what to do if you cannot use the app, see Account deletion.
  • Deleted data may remain in the infrastructure provider's automatic backups for up to 30 more days and is then deleted from the backups as well.

6. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and other laws that apply to you, you have the right to access, correct and delete your data and to object to its processing. You can view, edit or delete most of your data directly in the app. For other requests, contact us at the address below; requests are answered within 30 days.

7. Children

The app is designed for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. Significant changes are published on this page and the date at the top is updated.

9. Contact

For privacy questions and requests: mobilgaraj.dev@gmail.com
